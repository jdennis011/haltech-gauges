Alerts
The hub checks these against the live data ten times a second. While an alert's condition holds, the gauges show its message, switch to its face, or both, and go back when it ends. Where two alerts apply to the same gauge, the one higher in the list wins. Changes are saved as you make them.

Pick one from a widget's colour list, or write themecolour1 to themecolour8 in the JSON. Change one here and every widget using it changes with it, on every face of every gauge.

Drag a widget on the preview to move it; guides snap it to the face centre and to other widgets. Corner handles resize it (radius, width and height, or text size); the knob above rotates it (on a dial or ring, turns the scale). Arrow keys nudge it (shift for 10 px).
